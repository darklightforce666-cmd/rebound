# Browser regression steps

1. Check discovery, token detail, launch, portfolio, analytics, and docs at mobile and desktop widths. Fee copy must show 85% holders and 15% operations.
2. Search Second Wind. A 0.5 SOL buy at the default quote yields 4,700 WIND. Its 0.025 SOL creator fee allocates 0.02125 SOL to holders and 0.00375 SOL to operations.
3. Open Demo controls on the token screen. Fund a round. A claim before its hourly opening must fail. Advance one hour; the demo must pay the funded award automatically and increase SOL exactly once. A repeated claim must fail.
4. Sell 100 WIND and confirm. Future rewards end, while already-funded awards remain claimable. Check trade history shows the correct 0.0094 SOL quote amount.
5. Create a token through the three-step wizard. Empty fields and tax above 10% must fail. New tokens use SOL and the 85/15 policy.
6. Open the wallet popup on desktop and mobile. Check Phantom/Solflare selection, absent-provider feedback, close button, Escape dismissal, and demo continuation.
7. Verify removed navigation labels and retired project references are absent. Open the standalone preview and check the logo and banner PNG exports.
